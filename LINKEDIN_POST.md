We built an AI travel agent that deliberately breaks your trip — then repairs it live.

TravelPilot doesn't just plan itineraries. It scores how fragile your plan is before anything goes wrong (0-100 resilience score), then stress-tests it with Chaos Mode. Hit the button, watch an activity get cancelled, and see the system repair the itinerary with a surgical diff — not a full regeneration.

The scheduler is a real algorithm (greedy nearest-neighbor with Haversine distances), not an LLM guessing at ordering. The LLM handles intent parsing and explanations. The code handles optimization. That distinction matters.

Most AI travel demos show a perfect happy path. We showed the broken one — and made it fix itself.
